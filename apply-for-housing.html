01 — Before you apply

Find the right housing option.

Columbia Housing offers affordable housing and rental assistance in Columbia, Cayce, and surrounding communities.

Each program or property can have different requirements and application dates. Start with the official notice for the opportunity you are considering.

  • Public housingExplore affordable rental homes and community-based opportunities.
  • Housing Choice VouchersLearn about rental assistance in the private market, also called Section 8.
02 — Eligibility & preparation

Get ready to apply.

Review the requirements before opening the application form.

01

Choose a program

Check whether the property or program matches your household size and needs.

02

Check eligibility

Review income limits, age restrictions, and any residency rules in the official notice.

03

Check the deadline

Note the opening and closing date, the Eastern Time deadline, and what the notice asks you to prepare.

03 — Housing waitlists

Find a waitlist.

Explore how different waitlist statuses work, then check the official listing for current availability.

04 — Application steps

What happens after you apply?

Follow the instructions in the official notice and keep the credentials you used to apply.

  1. 01
    Keep your sign-in details Save your username and password in a secure place.
  2. 02
    Watch for correspondence Follow the agency’s directions for notices and next steps.
  3. 03
    Keep your details current Contact Columbia Housing for instructions if your information changes.
